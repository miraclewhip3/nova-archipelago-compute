Field notes

Sols
Build whatever you can afford, then press End sol. Production, upkeep and research run once per sol. Fewer sols to Living World is a better result.
Build zone
The dashed line. Every structure extends it; Relay Pylons reach 5 hexes and Drone Hubs 4. Dim land is unsurveyed.
Efficiency
Structures run at reduced output when power, oxygen or crew fall short, and when morale drops below 50. Check the meters before you build.
Upkeep
Every housed crew member eats rations each sol. Hungry or short of air, crews lose morale fast. On Standard and Hard, morale stuck at zero evacuates the colony.
Hazards
Dust storms cut solar (Battery Banks help). Cold snaps halve farms without an adjacent Heater. Meteors knock structures offline (Point Defense, or click to repair).
Unlocks
Early milestones unlock the first buildings and the Research Lab. After that, research unlocks everything else. Its tiers open at terraforming stages.
Monoliths
Cyan ? signals. Survey one for a research breakthrough and permanent Ti.
Controls
Drag or WASD pan · Wheel zoom · 1–9 pick · Space end sol · R research · M music · H lander · ? notes · Esc or right-click cancels

Nova Archipelago

A barren water world, and a lander on a chain of islands. Bring the planet from bare rock to a living world in as few sols as you can.

  • 1Build from the menu inside the dashed zone. Placement decides output, and every copy of a structure costs more than the last.
  • 2End the sol to run production. Terraformers add Ti every sol, but only at the efficiency your power, oxygen, crew and morale allow.
  • 3Keep crews fed and breathing. Prepare for dust storms, cold snaps and meteors.
  • 4Unlock new structures through early milestones, then a research tree that opens with each terraforming stage.
